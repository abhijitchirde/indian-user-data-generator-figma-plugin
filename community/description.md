## Tagline (short description)

Pick one:

1. Realistic Indian users for your designs, in one click.
2. Indian names, addresses, Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and more: fill cards, tables or JSON in one click.
3. Fake Indian user data that makes sense: the email matches the name, the address matches the city.
